### **Watch for Signs of Irritation:**

- ❌ Excessive dryness
- ❌ Peeling or flaking

### **If Irritation Occurs:**
1. **Reduce frequency** - Use combined version 2x/week instead
2. **Reduce amount** - Use 1-2 drops of Real C instead of 2-3
3. **Use separately** - Use Bio Serum alone on some days
4. **Take break** - Stop for 1 week, then restart slowly
