## ⚠️ IMPORTANT NOTES

- **You have TWO Vitamin C products** - Use ONE per day (alternate or choose your favorite)
- **NEVER use Retinal + AHA on the same day** - Alternate nights
- **Start slowly** - If new to actives, begin 2-3x/week and increase gradually
- **Always use SPF** in the morning (not included in your products - you need to add this!)

---

## 🌅 MORNING ROUTINE (Every Day)

### Step 1: Cleanser
- Use a gentle cleanser (not in your products - add one)
- **Time**: 1-2 minutes

### Step 2: Toner (Optional)
- Hydrating toner if you have one
- **Time**: 1 minute

### Step 3: Vitamin C Serum ⭐
**Choose ONE per day:**

**Option A: Bio Serum Vitamin C + E + Ferulic** (Recommended)
- Apply 3-4 drops to face and neck
- Gently pat in
- Wait 5-10 minutes before next step
- **Why**: Contains ferulic acid + vitamin E (like Skinceuticals formula!)

**Option B: Clínical The Real C drops (20%)**
- Apply 3-4 drops
- Gently pat in
- Wait 5-10 minutes
- **Why**: Higher concentration (20%), pure ascorbic acid

**Recommendation**: Use Bio Serum most days (better formula), use Real C 2x/week for extra boost

### Step 4: Moisturizer
- Lightweight moisturizer (not in your products - add one)
- **Time**: 1 minute

### Step 5: SUNSCREEN ⚠️ CRITICAL
- **SPF 50+ broad-spectrum** (MUST ADD THIS!)
- Apply generously
- Reapply every 2 hours if in sun
- **This is NON-NEGOTIABLE** when using Vitamin C and Retinal!

## 🌙 EVENING ROUTINE

### Step 1: Double Cleansing
- **Oil cleanser** (if wearing makeup/sunscreen)
- **Water-based cleanser**
- **Time**: 3-4 minutes

### Step 2: Toner (Optional)
- Hydrating toner
- **Time**: 1 minute

### Step 3: Active Treatment (Choose ONE per night)

#### **MONDAY & THURSDAY: Retinal 0.1** ⭐
- Apply pea-sized amount to face and neck
- Avoid eye area
- Wait 20-30 minutes before next step
- **Start**: 2x/week, increase to 3-4x/week if tolerated
- **Do NOT use with AHA on same day**

#### **TUESDAY & FRIDAY: AHA Complex 10%** ⭐
- Apply thin layer to face
- Avoid eye area and any active breakouts
- Leave on 10-20 minutes (start with 10 min)
- Rinse off with water
- **Start**: 1-2x/week, increase to 2-3x/week if tolerated
- **Do NOT use with Retinal on same day**

#### **WEDNESDAY, SATURDAY, SUNDAY: Rest Days**
- Skip actives OR use Bakuchiol cream (gentle alternative)
- Focus on hydration

### Step 4: Bakuchiol Well-Aging Cream
- Apply as final step on rest days (Wed, Sat, Sun)
- OR use as moisturizer after Retinal/AHA (wait 20-30 min after actives)
- Contains bakuchiol (retinol alternative) - gentle enough for daily use
- **Time**: 2 minutes

### Step 5: Eye Cream (Optional)
- Apply around eye area
- **Time**: 1 minute

## ⚠️ SAFETY RULES

### ❌ NEVER DO THIS:
1. ❌ Retinal + AHA on same day
2. ❌ Retinal + Vitamin C at same time (use Vitamin C morning, Retinal evening)
3. ❌ AHA + Vitamin C at same time (use Vitamin C morning, AHA evening)
4. ❌ Skip sunscreen (especially with Retinal!)
5. ❌ Use Retinal/AHA around eyes
6. ❌ Start all actives at once

### ✅ ALWAYS DO THIS:
1. ✅ Use SPF every morning
2. ✅ Wait 20-30 min after Retinal before moisturizer
3. ✅ Start slowly (2x/week for actives)
4. ✅ Listen to your skin - if irritated, take rest days
5. ✅ Patch test new products
6. ✅ Use gentle cleanser

---

## 🚨 SIGNS OF OVERUSE (Stop and Rest!)

- Redness or burning
- Excessive dryness or peeling
- Stinging when applying products
- Increased sensitivity
- Breakouts or irritation

**If this happens**: Stop all actives for 1-2 weeks, use only gentle cleanser, moisturizer, and SPF.

## 💡 PRO TIPS

1. **Vitamin C Strategy**: Use Bio Serum daily (better formula), use Real C 2x/week for extra boost
2. **Retinal Timing**: Apply to dry skin, wait 30 min before moisturizer for best results
3. **AHA Timing**: Start with 10 minutes, increase to 20 if tolerated
4. **Bakuchiol**: Perfect for rest days - gives anti-aging benefits without irritation
5. **Storage**: Keep Vitamin C products in cool, dark place (refrigerate if possible)
6. **Check Oxidation**: If Vitamin C turns dark yellow/brown, it's oxidized - replace it
